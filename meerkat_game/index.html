Informed Consent & Briefing

Study Title: Behavioral Ecology and Decision Sensitivity in Suricata suricatta Simulations.

Purpose: This research investigates the trade-off between foraging efficiency and anti-predator vigilance. You are acting as a proxy for a meerkat in a high-risk environment.

Procedures:

  • Movement: Use Arrow Keys to navigate the environment.
  • Foraging: Hold SPACE to dig for scorpions. Visual "whisker" rings indicate your search radius. As a patch depletes, your sensory radius shrinks, making it harder to find remaining scorpions.
  • Patches: You must decide when to leave a depleted area by clicking "MOVE TO NEW PATCH" or pressing P. This incurs a metabolic cost and requires traveling time.
  • Vigilance: Move to the Sentry Rock (Right side) to scan for predators. Predators are invisible (audible only) unless you are on the rock. Press S while on the rock to sound an alarm. Another community member may sometimes watch from the left rock, but their reliability may vary.
  • Survival: Your mob (25 members) depends on vigilance. Failure to spot predators results in a risk of a mob member being snatched. Risk scales with group size.

Confidentiality: All performance data is anonymized. By clicking below, you consent to provide behavioral data.
